Dear Shikha,

Sometimes I can be silly, stubborn, and not very good at expressing what's actually in my heart.

I know that sometimes my words or reactions hurt you, and I'm genuinely sorry for that.

I don't want you to feel that you have to fight to be understood by me. I want to learn to listen to you, understand your feelings, and make you feel safe enough to tell me anything.

I know love isn't just about the happy moments, cute pictures, gifts, or saying I love you. It's also about how we treat each other when we're upset, when we disagree, and when things aren't going our way.

I may not always get everything right, but I never want to stop trying to become a better partner for you.

I don't want a perfect relationship. I just want us to keep choosing each other, understanding each other, and loving each other a little better every day.

No matter how silly our fights get, I hope we never forget that we're on the same team.

You and me, Shikha. Always. ❤️
